About BUP

Established on June 5, 2008, Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP) is a public university run by the Bangladesh Armed Forces. Located in Mirpur Cantonment, Dhaka, BUP aims to facilitate professional degrees and to run undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate programs through its faculties and affiliated institutes.
